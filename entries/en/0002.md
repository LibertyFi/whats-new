# What's new in June 2026

A roundup of recent improvements across the platform — new features, polish, and fixes.

## New features

### New onboarding questionnaire

A redesigned onboarding questionnaire helps us understand your goals from day one, so we can tailor your experience and recommendations right from the start.

### Refreshed sidebar menu

The main sidebar has a new look and structure, making it easier to navigate between sections of the app at a glance.

### Playbooks promoted to the main sidebar

**Playbooks** now have a dedicated spot in the main sidebar, putting your reusable project templates one click away.

### New share & publish flow

Sharing and publishing your experiences has been reworked into a clearer, more guided flow — making it easier to get your content in front of viewers.

### Change main media directly in the video editor

You can now swap a slide's main media for new media without leaving the video editor — a faster, more fluid way to iterate on your videos.

### Duration control for intro & closing transitions

Intro and closing transition templates now expose a duration control, giving you finer control over the pacing of your video.

### Auto-detect language in Playbooks

Playbooks now offer an **Auto** option in the language selector, so the language adapts automatically to your project's content.

## Improvements

### Segmented buttons for text alignment

The text alignment controls in the list tab are now displayed as segmented buttons — clearer, faster, and easier to scan.

### Color picker enhancements

The color picker has received a round of refinements, making color selection more precise and pleasant to use.

## Fixes

- **Delete button in Avatar no longer opens the editor** — clicking delete now does exactly what it says.
- **Themes skip the main image cleanly** — applying a theme on slides without a main image no longer causes layout issues.
- **Multiple document uploads** — uploading several documents at once now works reliably.
- **Cloned voice names** — long voice names are no longer truncated in the voice list.
- **Brandkit changes now apply in the video editor** — edits made in the brandkit are immediately reflected in the video editor.
- **Duration slider capped correctly** — the duration slider no longer allows values above the asset's actual max length on video assets.
- **Correct seat pricing** — seat pricing now displays with the right decimal point (no more misplaced amounts).
- **Projects no longer get stuck** in the review flow.
- **Analytics back button** — going back from analytics now returns you to the correct previous page instead of looping.
- **Voice previews** — only one voice preview can play at a time now; selecting a new voice automatically stops the previous one.
- **Storyboard approval** — fixed an issue that could cause storyboard approval to fail with the wrong asset reference.
