# What's new in November 2026

A roundup of recent improvements across the platform — new features, polish, and fixes.

## New features

### Analytics by version

When you edit an add-on or the video and publish again, your analytics no longer mix the old and new content. The Add-ons and Video analytics tabs now have a **version selector**:

- It lists each version your viewers saw, with its date, its visits and a marker on the live one, followed by Preview and All versions. The live version is shown by default.
- Cards that always count every version say so with an All versions tag.
- The selector is hidden when viewers have only seen one version.

### Live activity across your organization

You can now follow your viewers in real time from the top of your list pages and project analytics pages:

- A **live viewers** badge shows how many people are viewing your experiences right now, across the whole organization. "Active now" on the Analytics page uses the same organization-wide count.
- The notifications bell now lists every live event: viewer peaks, milestones, trending experiences, chatbot answers and quiz completions with their score. Each notification shows the experience title and opens the matching analytics or chat history.
- Notifications and their read state are kept when you move between pages.

### Sound for intro and closing videos

In the storyboard editor, intro and closing scenes that use a video now have a **Play video sound** switch. When it is on, the video's own soundtrack plays instead of the background music, in the editor preview too. The switch is only available for videos that have a soundtrack, so generation no longer fails on a silent video.

### Directory group mappings

Organizations that sign in with SSO now have a **Group mappings** tab in their organization settings. Map each group from your identity directory to one or more roles so its members get the right access automatically. A clear message tells you when the directory sync fails.

## Improvements

### A refreshed look

The platform now has a cleaner, more consistent design:

- The **Libertify logo** is sharper in the sidebar, the mobile header and the experience page header.
- The sidebar has more even spacing, clearer menu text and a tidier user menu.
- On an experience page, a breadcrumb (Experiences › experience name) replaces the Back button, and the logo takes you home.
- Top-level pages such as Experiences, Profile and Tutorials share one layout, with a breadcrumb, a title and the same spacing.
- Cards, panels and dialogs now use the same rounded corners everywhere.

### Project analytics redesign

The **project analytics** page has a new layout:

- A banner with the project image and title, followed by four key figures for the selected dates: total views, average read depth, median drop-off and completion rate.
- The tabs now sit in one card, with the insights panel beside them on every tab. The Activity tab is now called Viewers, and the video analytics appear under Add-ons, or in their own Video tab when the project has no other add-on.
- Each step of the Reading Funnel now has a tooltip that explains it, and the Impression step now also counts viewers who open an experience without scrolling.

### Chat history within reach

A **View history** link on the experience's Chat card and on the Chat analytics tab now opens the chat history directly. The Back button of the chat history page returns you to the page you came from.

### Smoother chatbot answers

In the chat of the new experience page, **chatbot answers** now appear progressively as they are written, instead of in large chunks.

### Faster jumps to highlights

On the new experience page, the document now **jumps straight** to a highlight several pages away instead of scrolling through every page.

## Academics

### More Standout styles

Standouts can now be drawn as **brackets** or **strike-through**, in addition to highlight, underline, circle and box. For brackets, you choose which sides to show.

### Regenerate a failed video

When video generation fails, the Video tab of the experience page now says so once and shows a **Regenerate Video** button, so you can start again without leaving the page. Viewers don't see the button.

### Fixes for educators

- **Add-on generation** — while an add-on is generating, you can no longer start the same generation again by toggling its visibility, so your credits are no longer charged twice.
- **Podcast tab** — the loader now stays until the new episode can play, and "Podcast ready" only appears once a new episode is available.
- **Playbooks** — saving a playbook that has a visual source turned on no longer shows an error when Advanced settings are collapsed.
- **Video card** — on the Edit experience page, the Video card now says the video hasn't been generated yet instead of showing editor links.
- **Knowledge Hub analytics** — the project title in the analytics header now opens the Knowledge Hub experience instead of the classic project page.

## Fixes

- **Free experiences count** — the number of free experiences left is now correct right after you create one.
- **Analytics Back button** — it now returns to the page you opened analytics from, even after you move between analytics pages.
- **Experiences table in Analytics** — every row now shows its views, plays and documents, on every page of the table and in the Experiences list.
- **Chat input** — the box now grows with long questions instead of overflowing and covering the suggestions.
- **Share modal** — switching between People and Domain now clears the other field, and Save stays disabled while the typed email is invalid.
- **Document uploads** — file names containing #, ? or & no longer break the upload.
- **Access forms** — a wrong password or PIN, or a request from an email without access, now shows an error message in the player.
- **French chatbot disclaimer** — its grammar is fixed.
