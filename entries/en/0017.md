# What's new in November 2026

A roundup of recent improvements across the platform — new features, polish, and fixes.

## New features

### Publication rules for your organization

Organization owners now have **publication settings** in the organization preferences:

- Choose which access modes your experiences may use, and the mode new experiences start in.
- Set a maximum publication duration in days, or leave it empty for no limit.
- The launch modal, the Share modal and the create experience flow then only offer the allowed modes, and end dates can't go past the limit, including the expiration time in playbooks.

The Share modal and the create experience flow also have a new optional **Link expires on** field, so you can pick when a link stops working.

### Webhooks

Organization admins now have a **Webhooks** tab in the organization settings. Create, edit and delete webhooks for all events or a selection, rotate their secret, send a test delivery, and browse the delivery history with each delivery's payload.

## Improvements

### Clearer AI notices for viewers

Chats now start with a **"You're chatting with an AI assistant"** notice, kept just above the message box in the new experience chat so it never scrolls away. Embedded experiences also show "Generated with AI" next to "Powered by".

### Safer republishing

When you change the settings of a live experience from the launch modal, your **live link** now stays online while the new settings are saved. If a publication is refused, you now see why.

### Brand kit color for chat sources

In the classic experience page, the passages a chat answer points to in the document are now highlighted in your **brand kit's highlight color** when it has one.

### Cookie consent banner

The **cookie banner** has a cleaner design, and Accept and Reject now carry the same weight.

## Academics

### Start with a playbook

New users now get an education experience built with the **default Libertify playbook** from their very first document or prompt.

### Empty add-on warning

When you delete the last item of a Standouts, Quick Recall, Quiz or Key points add-on, a **warning** now tells you the add-on is empty and offers to hide it.

### LMS document upload

The **upload area** used when adding content from your LMS has a cleaner design.

### Fixes for educators

- **Editors opened from Moodle** — opening the video editor, the highlights editor or brand kit creation from an experience embedded in Moodle now works in the new tab instead of showing a login page.
- **Expired Moodle sessions** — editor tabs opened from an expired Moodle session now show the session-expired page instead of the login page.
- **Students on Safari** — students can now use an experience embedded in Moodle in Safari without losing their session.

## Fixes

- **Sign-in after logging out** — signing back in now opens the home page right away instead of sending you back to the login page.
- **Video highlights in documents** — highlights now appear exactly when the video reaches them, without a short delay.
