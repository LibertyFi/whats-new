# What's new in October 2026

A roundup of recent improvements across the platform — new features, polish, and fixes.

## Improvements

### Apply avatar and logo settings to every scene

You can now **apply avatar and logo settings** to every scene type, not only some of them. The avatar stays inside the frame when its width changes, and each chapter keeps its own presenter.

### Two more tutorial lessons

The **Tutorials** page now includes the Teacher DNA and editing-a-video lessons that were marked coming soon, and every lesson plays from YouTube.

## Academics

### Several documents from Moodle

From the LTI deep link in Moodle, teachers can now **upload several documents** at once — up to 35 — stage them, then create the experience when they are ready.

### Quiz edit card

Quiz questions can now be **reviewed and edited** from the Edit experience page: change the wording, the four answers and which one is correct, the same way you already edit Quick Recall.

### Video add-on

Hiding the **Video add-on** now removes it from the experience page. Turning it on for an experience that has no video yet starts generation.

### Expired Moodle session

When a Moodle session expires, teachers and students now see a **dedicated page** asking them to reopen the activity, instead of being sent to the regular login.

### Fixes for educators

- **Playbook settings** at creation are no longer overridden: a playbook without its own language is no longer forced to English, and the playbook's generation settings are the ones that apply.

## Fixes

- **Pronunciation preview and script playback** now use the current scene's voice, instead of a previously cached one.
- **Script and storyboard edits** now mark the video as out of date, so you are prompted to regenerate after a real content change.
