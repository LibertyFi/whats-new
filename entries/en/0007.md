# What's new in July 2026

A roundup of recent improvements across the platform — new features, polish, and fixes.

## New features

### Spanish (es-ES) locale

Libertify is now available in **Spanish** across the admin, media-player, and sales apps.

### Organization analytics overhaul

Org-level analytics have been significantly expanded:

- **Insights panel** on the organization analytics page.
- **Sessions & Unique Visitors** displayed as a nested bar chart.
- **Activity tab** on the project analytics page (Viewer analytics).
- **Refreshed UI** for both the organization analytics and experiences tables.

### Billing history & Stripe pricing table

Two big billing upgrades:

- Subscription plans now use the official **Stripe pricing table** instead of custom cards.
- A new **billing history** table lets you view past invoices and download them directly.
- An internal admin page lets our team **manage subscriptions by account**.

### CTAs & Share in the new Experience

**Calls-to-action** are now wired throughout experience v2 (both admin config and media-player runtime), and the **Share** button in the new experience is now live.

### Hey Genius preview is back

The **Hey Genius Preview** is back, with non-applicable features hidden from the Hey Genius project page for a cleaner focus.

### Avatars on transitions

You can now enable avatars on **transition scenes** and use **bulk actions** to assign avatars across transitions in one go.

### Mobile video speed selector

Viewers can now pick a **playback speed** for videos on mobile, matching the desktop experience.

## Improvements

### Readable Document reading analytics

The Document reading analytics page has been reworked for far better readability, making patterns easier to spot at a glance.

### Smarter Asset Library uploads

Uploading to the asset library now lets you set **tags and names** on the fly, and duplicate filenames on multi-upload are handled cleanly.

### Brand kit uploads on drop

Brand kit assets are now **uploaded immediately on drop** in the brandkit editor, instead of waiting until you hit Save — no more losing uploads to accidental navigation.

### Audio tab redesign

The Audio tab in the video editor has been redesigned, with brandkit music now properly applied.

## Fixes

- **Chat history date select** now works reliably.
- **Video player crash** from a stale ready-callback race has been fixed.
- **Missing text in videos** — text no longer disappears when a theme references a brandkit font that isn't defined in the brandkit.
- **Uploader hidden behind gallery** — the uploader in the storyboard editor's Objects/Media tab is now accessible again.
- **HeyGenius preview** now scrolls correctly to highlights.
- **Selected Music option** in the video editor is now properly highlighted with its selected color.
