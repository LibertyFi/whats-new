# What's new in October 2026

A roundup of recent improvements across the platform — new features, polish, and fixes.

## New features

### Password and email access for experiences

The Share modal and the **Access & sharing** card on the Edit experience page now let you choose who can open an experience:

- **Public** — anyone with the link.
- **Password** — viewers enter a password of at least 8 characters that you set and confirm.
- **Email** — only the emails or domains you list can get in. Invalid entries are rejected, and you need at least one recipient before saving, so you can't lock everyone out by mistake.

Protected links now show the matching PIN or password screen instead of a "not found" page.

### Insights for each project

The **Insights panel** is now available on each project's analytics Overview tab, not only at the organization level. A new Analytics button in the experience header takes you straight there.

## Improvements

### Document tabs on the experience page

When an experience has several documents, you now switch between them with **tabs** instead of a dropdown, and the header shows the project title. Extras stay hidden from the tabs and from chatbot sources.

### Tutorials sorted into categories

The **Tutorials** page is now split into Education and Video categories, matching our YouTube playlists, and adds the student onboarding and navigating-an-experience lessons.

### Chinese and Cantonese scripts

The Animation, Pronunciation and Emotions editors now **split Chinese and Cantonese scripts into words**, so you can select and edit words in these languages as you do in others.

## Academics

### View and edit your DNA

The DNA card on your profile now has **View details** and **Edit my DNA** buttons. View your archetype with its tagline and personality traits, or answer the onboarding questions again to recalculate your DNA.

### Key points edit card

The **Key points** of an experience can now be reviewed and edited from the Edit experience page, like Quiz and Quick Recall.

### Quiz difficulty

Quiz settings now have a **Difficulty** choice: Automatic, Simple, Balanced or Expert. Automatic, the default, follows your DNA. The number of questions is now set for you, so that setting is gone.

### Regenerate out-of-date add-ons

When an add-on is out of date, for example after you change its settings, the experience pages now offer **Regenerate add-ons**. Existing content stays visible until the new version is ready.

### Add-ons turned on later

Showing an add-on that was never generated on the Edit experience page now **starts its generation**:

- The Study Chatbot shows a loading state until it is ready, and an error state if generation fails.
- The Podcast asks you to pick a host and a guest voice first, instead of failing.

### Knowledge Hub and chatbot panels

The **Knowledge Hub** opens on its first tab with the chatbot collapsed. Clicking an add-on tab expands the hub on that add-on and collapses the chatbot.

### Formatted chatbot answers

The **Study Chatbot** now shows formatted answers with lists, bold text and line breaks, instead of raw markup.

### Tutorial for Moodle students

The first time a student opens an experience from Moodle, a **tutorial video** opens to show them around. A Tutorial button in the header lets them watch it again at any time.

### Fixes for educators

- **Share button** — viewers coming from Moodle no longer see a Share button that did nothing, and sharing options are hidden for LTI experiences.
- **Study Chatbot added after creation** — it now answers right away, instead of saying the documents are not ready until you publish again.

## Fixes

- **Storyboard saves** on projects with many assets no longer create duplicate storyboards.
- **Scenes** are no longer lost when an autosave and an add, remove or reorder of scenes happen at the same time.
- **Transition duration** — the slider is back on Flash projects and the duration you set is saved.
- **Most viewed project** — the home page no longer shows an error when that project has been deleted.
- **Classic Share modal** — it now opens on the access mode you saved, instead of always on Public.
- **Analytics after cookie consent** — page views are now counted once a viewer accepts cookies.
