# What's new in July 2026

A roundup of recent improvements across the platform — new features, polish, and fixes.

## New features

### Org-level analytics dashboard

A new **Overall Analytics** dashboard gives you an org-wide view of your experiences, with a list of all experiences and dedicated tabs to navigate between **Document**, **Video**, and **Chat** analytics.

### Richer video analytics panel

The video analytics panel now uses timestamps with chapter milestones, position charts, refined card tooltips, color semantics, and a smoother seek pattern for clearer, more actionable insights.

### LTI integration — Teacher & Student Auth

Libertify now integrates with **LTI**, with full teacher and student authentication flows so you can plug directly into your LMS. Publication mode is set at project creation to match your LTI context.

### Teacher onboarding questionnaire

A dedicated **teacher onboarding questionnaire**, wired into the LTI flow, helps educators get set up faster and lets us tailor the product to their classroom needs.

### Read chapter titles aloud

An option is now available to have chapter titles read aloud during transitions — helpful for accessibility and for narrating longer videos.

### Theme gallery landing view

The theme editor now opens on a new **theme gallery landing view**, making it easier to browse and pick a starting point for your video's look and feel.

### Image & video crop masks

You can now crop images and videos in the storyboard editor with **rectangle** and **circle** masks, unlocking cleaner, more designed layouts.

### Per-chapter avatars

Assign different avatars per chapter directly in the storyboard editor — perfect for multi-speaker or multi-topic videos.

### Cookie banner — Reject and Revoke

The cookie banner now includes a clear **Reject** option, and users can revoke their cookie consent later — giving your visitors full control and helping you stay compliant.

## Improvements

### First scene protection & inline whole-script editing

The video editor now protects your first scene from accidental deletion, merges narration cleanly, and lets you edit the whole script inline in one place.

### Language-aware font selection

Font selectors now display only the fonts that support the selected language, preventing missing-glyph surprises.

### Better item transforms & text rendering

Item rotation has been improved (migrated to react-moveable) and text rendering fidelity is much closer to the final output — what you see is what you get.

### Refined playbook brandkit settings

Playbook brandkit settings have been reworked for a clearer, faster configuration flow.

### Reorganized storyboard right panel & theme editor menu

The storyboard editor's right panel and the theme editor menu have been reorganized for better discoverability and quicker access to what you use most.

## Fixes

- **HTML entities no longer show literally** — apostrophes and ampersands now render correctly in translated UI text.
- **Storyboard upload → add to canvas** now works reliably even when a gallery asset path is missing.
