# What's new in June 2026

A roundup of recent improvements across the platform — new features, polish, and fixes.

## New features

### Zoom in & out on the selected slide

The video editor now lets you zoom in and out on the currently selected slide, making detailed editing far easier on dense layouts.

## Improvements

### Layers panel for non-Flash projects

New non-Flash projects now display layers from the template state in the Layers panel, giving you the same structured editing experience across project types.

### Cleaner Layers panel previews

Item text in the Layers panel is now shown on a single line with an ellipsis, keeping the panel tidy even with long titles.

### Video main image clearly labelled

When a slide's main image is a video, the Layers panel now shows a video icon and the video's name — no more guessing what's behind the thumbnail.

## Fixes

- **Duplicated Dashboard entry** in the menu has been removed.
- **Stray SLIDE tags** are no longer saved on storyboard assets without their association.
- **Phantom template saves** triggered by text auto-fit on load have been stopped, avoiding unnecessary writes.
- **Carousel scroll position** — loading-gated carousels now correctly scroll to the selected item instead of staying put.
