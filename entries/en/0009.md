# What's new in September 2026

A roundup of recent improvements across the platform — new features, polish, and fixes.

## New features

### Two-factor authentication

You can now secure your account with **two-factor authentication**, by SMS or with an authenticator app (TOTP). Add and verify your phone number, or scan a code with your authenticator app, and every sign-in will ask for a one-time code.

## Improvements

### More resilient experience pages

If one panel of the new experience page fails, the **problem stays contained** to that panel: the document, Knowledge Hub and chatbot keep working, and a **Try again** button reloads only the part that failed.

## Academics

Libertify for education has grown a lot over the last few releases. Here is the full picture of what teachers and students can use today, including features that shipped quietly in previous versions.

### Create experiences from playbooks

The new **Create experience** flow is built around playbooks. Pick one of Libertify's playbooks or one of your organization's own from the carousel, open its details to see the add-ons it includes, then customize it before creating.

- Upload your source documents right in the flow, split into **Source files** that students see and **Extras** that only feed the knowledge base.
- Customizing or choosing **Build your own** creates a draft playbook, and Libertify offers to save it so you can reuse it later.
- When **Create now** is not available yet, you are taken straight to the document upload instead of hitting a dead button.
- A playbook needs at least one visual source (AI images, document images, stock images or stock videos); saving one with none enabled now explains why.
- Admins choose between the playbook flow and the classic project wizard from every **Create experience** button.
- While creating or editing an experience, the admin sidebar steps aside to give you a focused, full-width workspace.

### The Knowledge Hub and its add-ons

Experiences created from a playbook open in the **Knowledge Hub**, a tabbed study space next to the document. Every add-on has its own configuration card when you create the experience, and can be revisited from the **Edit experience** page.

- **Video** — the generated lesson, with its own settings card; you can now generate a BrandKit right from that card.
- **Quiz** — auto-generated questions with configurable count, difficulty and answer reveal.
- **Quick Recall** — flashcards to review the key concepts (previously called Flashcards).
- **Key points** — an expandable summary organized by concept, with a choice of length, format and reading level (previously called Summary).
- **Standouts** — the passages that matter most, marked directly on the document.
- **Podcast** and **Study Chatbot** — see below.
- Add-on tabs show a **status indicator** while content generates and turn green when ready; only enabled add-ons are shown, and **scroll arrows** keep every tab reachable when the strip overflows.
- **General settings** such as language, the experience link, calls-to-action and access now live in their own card instead of inside the Video add-on.

### Podcast add-on

Turn a document into a **two-voice podcast**. Pick a voice for each of the two speakers when configuring the add-on, and listen to the episode directly in the Knowledge Hub's audio tab.

### Study Chatbot

The **Study Chatbot** answers questions about the experience's documents, both while you build the experience and for your viewers.

- Answers cite their sources as **reference cards**; clicking one jumps to the passage in the document, switching documents when needed.
- The chat shows a loading state until it is ready to greet you, with a retry if the connection fails.
- The chat and document panels have been redesigned.
- Chat settings such as the greeting, showing sources and suggested follow-up questions can be adjusted from the **Edit experience** page.

### Teacher onboarding and your pedagogical DNA

- Teachers from participating institutions are taken to a dedicated **teacher onboarding** after their first login, and skip the generic questionnaire.
- Your answers are saved as your **creator profile**, which shapes the content Libertify generates for you.
- The welcome step and the DNA questionnaire intro now fit inside the Moodle window without scrolling, and the intro video opens on click.

### LMS integration

- From the LTI deep link in Moodle, teachers can now **attach an existing experience** (any project that is Ready or Live) instead of uploading a new document.
- The new experience page has an **Export SCORM** button for organizations with SCORM export enabled, alongside the Share modal.
- SCORM tracking, including completion and resume, works on the new experience page as well as the classic one.

### Add-on analytics

Learner interactions with every add-on — quiz answers, Quick Recall ratings, Key points expands, Standout clicks, podcast and video plays — are now tracked and feed your experience analytics.

## Fixes

- **Chat** now scrolls to the latest message.
- **Chat on document-only experiences** connects on the first try instead of needing a retry.
- **PDF loading** no longer fails when a document's link refreshes, and the PDF engine now ships with the app for more reliable loading.
- **Document viewer** no longer reloads the PDF every few seconds while add-ons are generating.
- **Access & sharing** no longer shows a stale "no email" warning on experiences that used the removed restricted-access option.
- **Transition avatars** always speak their script; a visible avatar could previously be saved with empty speech.
