# What's new in October 2026

A roundup of recent improvements across the platform — new features, polish, and fixes.

## New features

### Share button for viewers

Viewers of a public experience can now **share it** from the Share button in the header. They can copy the link or send it by WhatsApp, email, Facebook, X or LinkedIn.

### Cookie choices everywhere

Your cookie choices, and your viewers', are now respected and easy to change:

- The platform now shows a **cookie banner**, and analytics only load once you accept. Change your choice at any time from **Cookie settings** in the profile menu. Your choice is saved to your account and applied when you sign in on another browser.
- Every experience page now has a **cookie settings button**, so viewers can withdraw their consent at any time. Rejecting clears the analytics identifiers already stored in their browser.
- Viewers are asked again after six months or when the cookie policy changes, and each choice is recorded.

## Improvements

### Privacy

- **Session recordings** are no longer captured, and error reports no longer include email addresses or link parameters.
- Two third-party analytics and testing tools have been removed from the platform, and analytics tags no longer load on pages that don't belong to a known experience.
- The password of a protected experience is now exchanged for a **secure token** instead of being kept in a cookie, and access codes from email links are no longer sent to analytics.

### Same languages in every selector

Every content language selector now offers the **same languages** as the project wizard, with names shown in your interface language.

## Academics

### Playbook flow for everyone

The playbook-based **Create experience** flow is now available to every user. Each Create experience button lets you choose between playbooks and the classic project wizard.

### Viewers pick up where they left off

Published experiences now **save each viewer's progress** in the add-ons and restore it on their next visit:

- **Video** and **Podcast** resume at the same position and speed.
- **Quiz** keeps the answers of the current attempt, and a retry starts fresh.
- **Quick Recall** remembers the cards already revealed and their ratings.
- **Key points** remembers which items were already opened.

### Edit Standouts

The Edit experience page now has a **Standouts** card next to the main document:

- Edit the list of standouts; changes save automatically.
- Click a standout to see its passage highlighted in the document.
- Select text in the document to create a standout or to change the passage of an existing one.
- Style each standout with its own color, thickness and padding.

### Edit an add-on from its tab

Each Knowledge Hub tab (Video, Key points, Quick Recall, Quiz, Podcast and Standouts) now has an **Edit** button that opens that add-on's card on the Edit experience page. The tabs themselves are also more compact.

### Edit experience page look

The **Edit experience page** now has the same look as the experience page. The Podcast card no longer shows a Duration setting.

### Fixes for educators

- **Regenerate** — changing the layout in General settings no longer marks every add-on as out of date, so regenerating no longer overwrites content you edited by hand.
- **Key points** — line breaks in paragraphs are now kept.
- **Knowledge Hub scrolling** — add-on content now scrolls inside its own tab, each tab opens at the top, and the bottom spacing is consistent.

## Fixes

- **Playback speed menu** — it no longer covers the video seek bar when you click just above the speed button.
- **Video editor themes** — when applying a theme fails, the selected slide's design is restored and no other slide is changed.
- **Extras** — hidden documents are now also left out of the document tabs and chatbot sources of published experiences.
- **Password-protected experiences** — an earlier access link no longer shows a wrong-password error after the experience switches to password access.
