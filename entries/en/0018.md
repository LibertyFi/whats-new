# What's new in November 2026

A roundup of recent improvements across the platform — new features, polish, and fixes.

## New features

### Time spent on each page

The Document tab of your project analytics now has a **Time per page** card, right under the key figures. It shows how many readers reach each page and how many seconds they spend on it on average, with a median line and the pages that hold attention longest highlighted.

- The Reading depth curve has been removed, and Scroll density now takes the full width.
- An "All time" tag marks the cards that don't follow the date picker.
- Reader segment names are now translated.

### Podcast analytics

You can now see how viewers listen to your **podcast**:

- In the Viewers tab, the Podcast column shows each viewer's listening time and latest playback speed under the bar, with a help icon that explains both values.
- The Add-ons tab has a Podcast pill with a listener retention chart and a seek patterns chart, and the podcast now appears in the add-ons overview with "listened to the end" as its completion rule.
- Projects whose only add-on is a podcast now open on the Add-ons tab.

## Improvements

### Subscription in your profile

Your **subscription** now lives in its own section of the profile page. It shows your plan, its status and dates, and your usage against each limit, with buttons to manage or cancel the subscription and to see your billing history. The Upgrade buttons on the home page now take you there.

### Sign-in and account dialogs

The **two-factor authentication** prompt has a new design, and the account locked, sign-in and delete account dialogs now share the same look. Sign-in error notifications are also easier to read.

### Live viewers badge

The **live viewers** badge in the top bar is now hidden when nobody is watching, instead of showing "0 viewers live".

### Video editor

The **Avatar tab** no longer appears when the Intro or Closing scene is open in the video editor, since the avatar doesn't apply to those scenes.

## Fixes

- **Reading analytics after switching documents** — scrolling is now still tracked after a viewer switches to another document in an experience.
- **Invitation roles** — when you invite members, the role list now only offers roles that match the workspace or organization you invite them to.
- **Restricted newsletter embeds** — an embedded newsletter you can't access now shows an access error instead of an empty player.
- **SCIM role hint** — the hint now explains that workspace roles apply only in the selected workspaces.
