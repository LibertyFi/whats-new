# What's new in September 2026

A roundup of recent improvements across the platform — new features, polish, and fixes.

## New features

### Tutorials

A new **Tutorials** page, reachable from your user menu, gathers short video guides on getting the most out of Libertify. Lessons still in production show up as coming soon.

### Update prompts after editing a published experience

When you change an experience after publishing it, the sharing panel now flags that the **publication is out of date** and offers an **Update** button, including on projects without a video add-on. When the video itself needs regenerating, a **Regenerate** notification appears instead.

## Improvements

### Change the avatar from a transition scene

The **Change avatar** button is now available on transition scenes too, since the presenter is shared with the chapter.

### Clearer replace-media hint

The storyboard editor's Objects tab now shows a visible notice explaining that clicking an asset replaces the selected image or video.

## Academics

### Your pedagogical DNA on your profile

The **profile page** now includes a DNA section showing the teaching archetype revealed during teacher onboarding, with its illustration.

### Playbooks

- Select **several goals** at once when customizing a playbook.
- Your organization's own playbooks in the create-experience carousel now have a menu to **edit, rename or delete** them; Libertify's official playbooks stay read-only.
- The save-playbook step now reads as an invitation, and nothing is stored until you confirm.
- The language setting offers **Auto**, detected from the document.

### Podcast

- The Podcast has its own redesigned card in the Knowledge Hub, separate from audio documents.
- Podcast settings can be edited from the **Edit experience** page, with voices listed by name and tone.
- **Auto-play on open** now works: when enabled, the episode starts as soon as the Knowledge Hub opens.

### Knowledge Hub

- Add-on tabs follow the **order you arrange** in the Edit experience rail.
- When Video is the only add-on, it takes over the Knowledge Hub header with its own name and icon, without a tab strip.
- The chatbot panel is now titled **Study Chatbot Assistance**.
- **Quick Recall** cards can be reviewed and edited from the Edit experience page.
- Document-first education projects now always open in the new experience page, whether from lists, cards or the video editor.
- **Standouts** are animated as they are drawn on the document, and they are marked on the first click rather than only scrolled to.

### Fixes for educators

- **Edit experience** no longer fails with a 404 when the requested experience instance cannot be found.

## Fixes

- **Video play overlay** in the storyboard editor is clickable again after adding a video to the canvas.
- **Unsaved-changes state** no longer carries over from one editing session to another project's autosave.
- **Video editor** keeps its live connection during long sessions, so text-to-speech no longer stops until you reload.
- **Pronunciation tab** now shows the current scene's script after moving between scenes.
